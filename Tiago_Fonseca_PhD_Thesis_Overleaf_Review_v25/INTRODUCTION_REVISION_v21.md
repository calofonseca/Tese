# Introduction Revision v21

This revision refines Chapter 1 after the structural compression introduced in v20.

## Changes

- Section 1.2 now states explicitly that the detailed state-of-the-art and residual-gap analysis is carried out in Chapter 2.
- Section 1.2 clarifies that scalability, deployability, safety, and robustness are the four dimensions investigated directly by the thesis, not a complete taxonomy of real-world CPS concerns. Cybersecurity/privacy, human factors/interpretability, regulation/markets, maintainability, and long-term organisational adoption are acknowledged but remain outside the primary research scope.
- Added a concise `Research Objectives` subsection under Section 1.3, with six objectives aligned to the thesis progression and contributions. No research-strategy or traceability subsection was restored.
- Renamed Section 1.4 from `Contributions` to `Research Contributions and Outcomes`.
- Expanded Section 1.5 (`Research Path and Thesis Organisation`) to explain the cumulative empirical progression, how earlier methods generated requirements for TI-MARL, and how systems/deployment work fed back into algorithm and simulator design.
- The chapter remains intentionally shorter than the pre-v20 introduction and avoids reintroducing the removed research-problem, terminology-table, publication, and authorship duplication.
