\frontmatterchapter{Declaration on the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}

Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) supported the work reported in this thesis, chiefly as a partner in the critical discussion of research, architecture, and implementation decisions. It was brought into the process while alternatives were still being considered, helping to question assumptions, compare possible designs, check consistency between requirements and technical specifications, and identify edge cases before changes reached the code. It also assisted with research organisation, drafting, language revision, documentation, software implementation, debugging, and testing.

Software-related assistance followed a specification-led process. Requirements and interfaces were established before implementation and served as the reference against which proposed designs and code were reviewed. For research and writing tasks, suggested interpretations, references, numerical values, and scientific claims were checked against primary sources, datasets, source code, tests, and experimental records. Generated material was rewritten or discarded whenever it did not meet these checks.

The author defined the research questions and methods, designed and verified the experiments, interpreted the results, and made all final decisions concerning the software and the manuscript. Responsibility for the originality, accuracy, arguments, conclusions, and final content of the thesis rests entirely with the author.
