\frontmatterchapter{Declaration on the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}

Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) supported the discussion of research and technical decisions throughout this work. Its use helped examine assumptions and compare alternative designs, particularly when translating research goals into software requirements and interfaces. It also assisted in identifying inconsistencies and edge cases before implementation. Further uses included research organisation, the preparation and revision of text, and support for software development, documentation, debugging, and testing.

Software development followed requirements and interfaces established before implementation, which provided the basis for reviewing proposed designs and code. Suggested references, numerical values, and interpretations were checked against the relevant primary sources and the project's data, code, tests, and experimental records. Material that did not withstand this review was revised or discarded.

The research questions and methods, experimental design and verification, and interpretation of results remained the author's responsibility, as did all final decisions concerning the software and the manuscript. The author is fully responsible for the originality and accuracy of this thesis, including its arguments and conclusions.
