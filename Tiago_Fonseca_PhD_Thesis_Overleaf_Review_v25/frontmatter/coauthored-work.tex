\frontmatterchapter{Statement on Co-Authored Work}

This thesis includes research developed in collaboration with supervisors, research colleagues, students, open-source communities, and industrial partners. Some methods, software components, datasets, experiments, and research outputs presented in the thesis are therefore associated with co-authored publications or shared research artefacts.

Where collaborative work contributes directly to a method, scientific claim, experiment, software component, dataset, or demonstrator presented in the thesis, the corresponding contributions and provenance are identified in the relevant chapter or section.

During the doctoral programme, I also supervised, co-supervised, and mentored a number of student theses and research projects developed alongside and, in some cases, contributing directly to this research. This work involved defining research problems, planning and reviewing technical work, coordinating parallel activities, and developing structured methods and pipelines for software development, experimentation, validation, documentation, and research follow-up. It provided substantial experience in leading and supervising research teams and in guiding work from an initial problem to a technically and scientifically defensible outcome. Where results or artefacts originating from these activities are used directly in the thesis, the corresponding student contribution and my role in their supervision, development, validation, and integration are stated in the relevant chapter.
