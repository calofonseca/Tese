\frontmatterchapter{Statement on Co-Authored Work}

The components presented in this thesis form part of a research architecture conceived and defined by the author. Several were developed in collaboration with supervisors, research colleagues, students, open-source contributors, and industrial partners. The author proposed and formulated the corresponding lines of work and contributed to their technical direction, development, supervision, and validation. Their integration into the wider project connected these collaborative efforts to the research questions addressed by the thesis.
