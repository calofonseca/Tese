More importantly, she continued to love and support me even when the pressure of this work made that support harder to give than I would like to admit.

A finished thesis inevitably presents research as something ordered: problems lead to methods, methods lead to experiments, and experiments lead to conclusions. Living through it is very different. Much of this journey was uncertain, repetitive, frustrating, and far less elegant than the document that eventually emerged from it. Inês was beside me throughout that real version of the PhD. Her patience, strength, affection, and belief in me made an enormous difference not only to completing this thesis, but to the person I remained while doing it.
